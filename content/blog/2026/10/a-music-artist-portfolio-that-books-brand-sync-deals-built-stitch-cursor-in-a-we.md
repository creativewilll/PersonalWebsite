# A Music Artist Portfolio That Books Brand Sync Deals — Built Stitch → Cursor in a Weekend

A music artist portfolio for brand sync is a clearance card. It names who can license the composition, who controls the recording, and which human answers the email.

I am William Spurlock, AI Systems Architect and Fractional AI CTO at Spurlock Studios LLC. I draw that card in Google Stitch, then I have Cursor ship the page. The weekend holds only when those names are already written down. If they are not, I stop. I do not invent a publisher.

This is not the fan site. Listeners and merch live in a different room. A supervisor with a picture and a deadline needs the owner lines on the first screen.

## What is a music artist portfolio for brand sync?

**A music artist portfolio for brand sync is a public page that lets a music supervisor see who grants the composition license, who grants the master license, and who picks up the phone.** The song can sound great and still be unusable if those three lines are missing.

The U.S. Copyright Office treats a song as two works. In its guide [How Songwriters, Composers, and Performers Get Paid](https://www.copyright.gov/music-modernization/educational-materials/musicians-income.pdf), revised July 2022, a musical work is the composition and a sound recording is the captured performance. Putting either one against picture needs its own license. The Office says a synchronization license covers the composition in timed relation to visual content, and a master use license covers the recording. It also says there is no compulsory sync license and no compulsory master use license. The mechanical license for audio-only copies does not cover a film, ad, or video, because phonorecords exclude sounds that accompany an audiovisual work. See 17 U.S.C. §§ 101 and 115 in that same note.

So the page is a desk, not a poster. I want a supervisor to forward three lines without opening a second tab.

The card has three jobs:

- Show the composition side and the recording side as separate lines, because the Copyright Office says they are separate works.
- Show a person, not a logo farm.
- Show only versions you can send the same day.

I do not put a price on that card. [ASCAP's checklist for film and other picture](https://www.ascap.com/help/career-development/a-checklist-for-using-music-in-film), which I read on October 4, 2026, says sync and master fees are negotiable and not a standard rate. The fee moves with the use, the length, how often the cue hits, and where the picture plays. A public number would be a guess. The page gets them to the person who can negotiate. It does not pretend to be the license.

## Why does a fan site fail a music supervisor?

**A fan site fails a music supervisor when the first screen sells a ticket or a shirt and never names the publisher or the master owner.** The supervisor is not shopping merch. They are trying to clear a cue before a picture locks.

I already shipped the fan room as its own site. That build is [the artist site aimed at listeners and merch](/blog/building-fordo-immersive-artist-website-ai-studio-cursor). Keep it. Do not ask it to do this job too. A supervisor who lands on a tour banner still has to email someone and ask who owns the song. That email is the delay.

ASCAP's checklist, read October 4, 2026, splits the paperwork the same way the Copyright Office does. The synchronization license comes from the copyright owner of the musical work, usually the publisher. The master use license comes from the owner of the specific recording, usually the label. [ASCAP's licensing FAQ](https://www.ascap.com/help/ascap-licensing), also read that day, says ASCAP does not license synchronization rights. Writers or publishers do. An ASCAP license also does not authorize reproduction of the sound recording. Membership in a PRO is not the clearance.

The fan page hides the deal in four common ways:

- The only contact is a link-in-bio with eight icons and no rights line.
- The page says "sync available" and then does not say who signs.
- The player starts the full master before anyone asked for it.
- The press kit is a zip from 2022 with no owner names inside.

I have a hard opinion here. If the artist cannot say who administers the composition, the site does not get a sync badge. A badge without a name wastes the supervisor's hour and makes the next email colder.

Motion still matters. The desk should feel like the records, not like a form from a county clerk. That visual system lives in [the immersive web manual](/blog/immersive-web-design-manual). The type can be big. The first facts still have to be the owner lines.

## Which fields belong on a music artist portfolio for brand sync?

**The fields on a music artist portfolio for brand sync are the song, the composition contact, the master contact, the identifiers you actually have, the versions you can send, and one human email.** Anything else is optional and goes below the fold.

The July 2022 Copyright Office guide is specific about identifiers. An ISWC identifies the musical work. An ISRC identifies the sound recording. The regional ISWC agency for the United States and Canada is operated by ASCAP, and you do not have to be an ASCAP member to get one, but you do have to be registered with a PRO. I print an ISRC or an ISWC only when the note already contains it. I do not mint one in the prompt.

Same person on both licenses is allowed to say so. The Office still counts two licenses. If the artist owns the composition and the master, the card can read "composition and master: [name]." If a publisher has the composition and a label has the master, the card shows two rows. I do not write "one-stop" as decoration when the rights are split.

Versions I will list, and only when the file is real:

- Main mix, the recording a supervisor would actually cut.

I leave TV-mix language off unless that file is in the folder. A label you cannot fulfill is worse than a short list.

What stays off the first screen:

- A price.
- A full bio longer than four lines.
- Autoplay.
- A shop.
- Every streaming icon before the owner lines.

ASCAP's checklist says a clearance request should include how the song is used, the length, the placement, and where the picture will be seen. Those facts belong in the supervisor's email, not in a fake rate card on the site. The page makes that email possible. It does not answer a brief that has not arrived.

I run this on my phone before I call the page done. Cursor's checkpoint is for the code. This test is for the desk.

## FAQ

### What is a synchronization license?

**A synchronization license is the right to use a musical work in timed relation with picture, and the U.S. Copyright Office says there is no compulsory sync license.** The [July 2022 guide](https://www.copyright.gov/music-modernization/educational-materials/musicians-income.pdf) says the composition owner negotiates that license in the market. A mechanical license for CDs, downloads, or audio streams does not cover a film, ad, or video. The portfolio should name the person who can grant it.

### What is a master use license?

**A master use license is the right to use a specific sound recording in picture, and it is separate from the sync license.** The same Copyright Office guide calls it the license to the recording, negotiated with the owner, with no compulsory master use license. [ASCAP's checklist](https://www.ascap.com/help/career-development/a-checklist-for-using-music-in-film), read October 4, 2026, says you clear that right with the owner of the recording, usually the label. The page needs that name even when the artist also wrote the song.

### Does an ASCAP membership replace a sync license?

**No. ASCAP membership does not replace a sync license.** [ASCAP's licensing FAQ](https://www.ascap.com/help/ascap-licensing), read October 4, 2026, says ASCAP does not license synchronization rights. Writers or publishers do. ASCAP also does not issue single-song licenses, and an ASCAP public-performance license does not authorize reproduction of the sound recording. Put the publisher on the card. Leave the PRO logo as a small mark if you want it at all.

### What is the difference between an ISWC and an ISRC?

**An ISWC identifies the musical work, and an ISRC identifies the sound recording.** The Copyright Office guide, revised July 2022, says the regional ISWC agency for the United States and Canada is operated by ASCAP, and labels or ISRC managers commonly assign ISRCs. I print either code only when it is already in the rights note. A prompt should not invent one.

### Can Google Stitch publish the production site by itself?

**No. Google Stitch does not publish this production site.** On March 18, 2026, Google Labs said Stitch turns natural language into high-fidelity UI, exports DESIGN.md, and can send designs to AI Studio and Antigravity. I use that handoff, then Cursor Agent edits the repo. The live page is the route in the codebase, not the Stitch canvas.

### Should a sync portfolio autoplay the master?

**No. A sync portfolio should not autoplay the master.** The supervisor often opens the page in a room with other people, and the first fact they need is the owner lines, which they can read with the sound off. A short clip they choose to start is enough. The full file goes out by email after someone asks.

### What belongs in the first email to a music supervisor?

**The first email should name the song, the composition contact, the master contact, and the versions you can send today.** ASCAP's checklist says a picture request should also describe the use, the length, the placement, and where it will be seen, but those details come from the supervisor's brief, not from a public price. Your email answers who can say yes. Their email answers how the cue is used.

### How is a sync desk different from a fan merch site?

**A sync desk names the two copyright owners, and a fan merch site sells to listeners.** I keep those as different pages. The listener build is [the fan site for merch and the live show](/blog/building-fordo-immersive-artist-website-ai-studio-cursor). The sync desk is the clearance card in this post. Mixing them puts a shop above the publisher line, which is the failure mode.

### When should the weekend build stop?

**Stop the weekend build when the rights note cannot name the composition contact or the master contact.** Stitch can still draw a dark card, and Cursor can still ship a route, but I will not fill the blank with a guessed company. Get the name, then run the three blocks. A page that says available and names nobody is not a portfolio for brand sync.
