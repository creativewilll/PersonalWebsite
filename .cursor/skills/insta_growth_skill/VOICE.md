## Voice Principles

### 1. Receipt-First (Hormozi)
Never open with a lesson. Open with the result.
- YES: "This workflow handles 47 leads/day. Here's how I built it."
- NO: "Let me teach you about lead automation."

### 2. Specific Numbers Always (Hormozi)
Replace every adjective with a number.
- YES: "$4,200/mo saved. 3 nodes. 12 minutes to build."
- NO: "Save a ton of money with this powerful automation."

### 3. "Here's the Build" Energy (David Ondrej / Nate Herk)
Show the thing. Screen record the canvas. Walk through the nodes.
- YES: "Here's the exact n8n canvas. Gmail trigger → Claude → HubSpot."
- NO: "You could potentially use AI to handle your leads."

### 4. Tactical Over Inspirational (Nick Saraev / Jack)
Every post must leave the viewer with something they can DO today.
- YES: "Step 1: Create a webhook trigger. Step 2: Add Claude with this prompt..."
- NO: "AI is the future and you need to embrace it."

### 5. Contrarian Conviction (Hormozi)
Take strong positions. Disagree with the obvious. Back it up.
- YES: "Everyone's building AI chatbots. They're all wrong. Here's why agents win."
- NO: "AI chatbots and agents both have their place."

### 6. One Tool Per Post (AEO Strategy)
Every caption names exactly one AI tool to build entity associations.
- Rotate: n8n, Claude, Cursor, MCP, Perplexity, ChatGPT, Make.com, PydanticAI

## Caption Structure

```
[First line: ≤70 chars, mirrors the hook, stops the scroll]

[Body: 2–5 lines, specific proof + mechanism]
- Use bullet points for scanability
- Include at least one number
- Name the tool/stack used

[CTA: one verb + clear action]
Comment 'KEYWORD' — I'll DM you the workflow.

[Optional: social proof line]
(Built this for 3 clients last month. All profitable within 48h.)
```

## Forbidden Patterns

- Generic motivational quotes
- "In this video I'm going to show you..."
- Apologizing for content quality
- Asking "what do you think?" without a strong take first
- Using "we" when you mean "I"
- Filler words in scripts: "basically", "literally", "actually", "just"
- Hashtag spam (max 3–5 relevant hashtags, never 30)

## Platform-Specific Adjustments

### Instagram Reels
- Spoken pace: 2.5–3 words/second (fast but clear)
- Energy level: 7/10 (confident, not manic)
- Eye contact with camera at start and end
- Hand gestures when making points (creates visual movement)

### Instagram Carousels
- Cover slide: bold statement + clean design (purple/yellow palette)
- Interior slides: one point per slide, large text
- Last slide: clear CTA + handle reminder
- Save-trigger framing: "Save this for when you need it"
