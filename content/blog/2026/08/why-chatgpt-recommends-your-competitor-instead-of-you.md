A competitor win is a readable event: the engine named someone, with or without a source, and that pattern maps to a failure mode. Guessing "we need more content" first polishes the wrong surface.

## Why does ChatGPT name a competitor when your site also answers the question?

**ChatGPT names a competitor because the shortlist is assembled from public evidence and match quality — not from "does this company's site contain an answer."** Your page can be correct, indexed, and even cited as a footnote while the model still hands the hire/buy slot to a rival whose name appears in more independent places, with cleaner facts, on the surfaces the retrieve (or the training memory) already trusts.

Owners open the homepage, confirm it "covers" the topic, and conclude the model is broken. OpenAI's [ChatGPT search](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/9237897-chatgpt-search) help page (fetched August 2026) says it may search when the question needs current information, then write an answer with links. It is deciding which *business names* are safe to recommend — not grading your page.

| Signal that usually won for the rival | What you saw in the answer | What it is not |
|---|---|---|
| **Repeated third-party naming** | Rival appears on directories, "best of" pages, review sites the answer echoes | Proof their site is prettier |
| **Cleaner entity** | One name, one city, one category, no DBA soup | A Wikipedia requirement |
| **Better prompt match** | Rival's public copy uses the buyer's nouns (city, job, constraint) | A secret ranking factor you cannot see |
| **Training-famous brand** | National chain named with no live links | Proof search ran |
| **Retrieved source pile** | Perplexity or ChatGPT citations point at the rival's profiles | Proof you were crawled and rejected |

I score the loss on three different events, the same split I use in [how to track when AI tools cite or recommend your business](/blog/how-to-track-when-ai-tools-cite-or-recommend-your-business):

| Event | Rival won if… | You still have a different problem if… |
|---|---|---|
| **Mention** | Their brand string appears | You are mentioned too; this is not a total miss |
| **Citation** | A URL or named source points at them | You are cited as a source but not picked |
| **Recommendation** | They are the hire/buy/shortlist option | You are in an "others include" dump |

A citation without a recommendation is a footnote. A recommendation without a citation is still a lost lead. Write down *which* event the rival took before you "fix the blog."

### The six competitor-win modes I actually use

Labels for the screenshot, not a 90-day plan:

1. **Missing entity** — the engine cannot resolve you as one business.
2. **Missing proof** — the engine can resolve you, but independent pages do not treat you as an option.
3. **Match miss** — you exist and have proof, but for a different category, city, or customer than the prompt.
4. **Memory miss** — search did not run (or ran thin); a well-known brand filled the gap.
5. **Retrieval miss** — search ran; the retrieved pile named the rival.
6. **Knowledge-base miss** — the rival is a documented public entity (often Wikipedia-class) and you are a private-reputation business.

If you published nothing on the topic, you do not have a competitor-win diagnosis. You have a blank page. This post assumes the page exists.

| If the answer looks like… | First mode to test |
|---|---|
| Rival named with a reason; you absent; no sources | Memory miss or missing proof |
| Rival named; Perplexity shows their directory/review URLs | Retrieval miss + missing proof |
| You named with the wrong city or a sister brand | Missing entity |
| You named as "also" after two rivals | Match miss or missing proof |
| Rival is a household name; local independents ignored | Memory miss |
| Rival has a Wikipedia article; you do not | Knowledge-base miss — test it; do not assume it |

ChatGPT does not publish a "why we picked them" log. Infer the mode from the answer, the citation list, and a one-hour check of the surfaces it already used.

## How do you tell a missing-entity problem from a missing-proof problem?

**A missing-entity problem means the machine cannot tell that your site, profiles, and mentions are one company. A missing-proof problem means it can tell who you are — it just does not have independent pages that treat you as a hireable option.** Those two fail the same prompt and need opposite first fixes. Mixing them is how you spend a month on schema while the rival keeps winning because three listicles never learned your name.

The cheap test is a brand prompt versus a category prompt, read as a pair. The [15-minute audit](/blog/is-your-business-invisible-to-ai-how-to-run-a-15-minute-ai-visibility-audit) owns timed pass/fail. Here you classify the competitor win.

| Prompt type | Missing entity looks like | Missing proof looks like |
|---|---|---|
| **"Who is [Your Brand]?"** | Wrong category, namesake collision, "I don't have information," invented address | Correct category and site |
| **"Best [job] in [market]?"** | Rival named; you absent *and* brand prompt also failed | Rival named; you absent; brand prompt passed |
| **Open-web search for your exact name** | Three legal names, two phones, a dead DBA | One clean identity, thin third-party pages |
| **Open-web search for the rival + your category** | Rival also messy — then entity is not why they won | Rival sits on directories and listicles you are missing |

If the brand prompt fails, stop diagnosing proof. You have an identity problem. The repair lives in [entity SEO: how to make your brand something AI actually knows](/blog/entity-seo-how-to-make-your-brand-something-ai-actually-knows). This section only needs you to *tell the two apart*.

A missing-entity problem can *look* like missing proof because messy names never accumulate under one string. Freeze the canonical name first. Do not count "Acme HVAC LLC," "Acme Heating," and "Acme Air" as three proof points. They are three entities.

Plenty of businesses fail both. The rule is still serial: brand prompt wrong → **entity first**; brand prompt right and category still names the rival → **proof first**; brand prompt right but the wrong job or city → **match miss**.

| Pattern this week | Mode | Wrong first move |
|---|---|---|
| ChatGPT describes you; never recommends you | Missing proof | Rewriting the homepage hero |
| ChatGPT confuses you with a namesake | Missing entity | Pitching another listicle under the wrong string |
| ChatGPT recommends you in the wrong city | Match miss | Buying a national PR hit |
| Perplexity cites your URL; ChatGPT names the rival | Split mode — see browse section | Treating both engines as one exam |

Proof is third-party naming — [digital PR for AI visibility](/blog/digital-pr-for-ai-visibility-getting-mentioned-where-ai-reads) owns that list. Do not call an identity collision a "visibility" problem.

## Does ChatGPT browse live, and how does that change the diagnosis?

**Yes — ChatGPT can search the web, but not on every turn, and that fork changes the diagnosis.** OpenAI's [ChatGPT search help page](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/9237897-chatgpt-search) (fetched August 2026) says it may search automatically when the question needs current information, that you can start search manually, that search is available on Free, Go, Plus, Pro, Business, Enterprise, and Edu (including signed-out), and that it can use location for local results. There is no official "always-on crawl of your homepage." Split the diagnosis on whether *this* answer used the live web.

| Clue in the answer | Browse likely | Browse unlikely / thin |
|---|---|---|
| Numbered or hoverable source links | Yes | — |
| Prices, hours, "open now," this week's news | Yes | — |
| Only household brands, no URLs, vague "well-known companies" | Maybe not | Memory filled the shortlist |
| You forced **Search** in the composer | Yes | Comparing search-off to search-on is a different exam |
| Enterprise workspace with web search disabled | No | Admin setting, not your SEO |

Enterprise and Edu owners can disable web search per the [Enterprise and Edu search page](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/10093903-chatgpt-search-for-enterprise-and-edu) (fetched August 2026). A locked-down work account is a different exam than chatgpt.com. Log plan and search state. Do not trend search-off Monday against search-on Friday.

### How the fork changes what you blame

| Search state | If the rival won | What you are actually diagnosing |
|---|---|---|
| **Search off / unavailable** | Famous brand or old training-famous name | Memory miss. Fresh pages you shipped last month may not exist in this answer. |
| **Search on, sources shown** | Rival's directories, reviews, or listicles cited | Retrieval miss. The open web already treats them as the option. |
| **Search on, no useful sources** | Rival still named | Weak retrieve *or* the model fell back to a known entity. Re-run once; do not overfit one chat. |
| **Search on, your URL cited, rival recommended** | They won the *name* slot | Proof / match, not crawlability. You were readable. You were not the pick. |

Perplexity is a different instrument. Its research post [Architecting and Evaluating an AI-First Search API](https://research.perplexity.ai/articles/architecting-and-evaluating-an-ai-first-search-api) (September 25, 2025) describes a first-party index that "tracks over 200 billion unique URLs," hybrid retrieval, and PerplexityBot. A Perplexity loss with visible citations is a retrieval-and-proof diagnosis, not a browse mystery.

Google AI Overviews and Gemini sit on a third path. [AI features and your website](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/ai-features) (Search Central, fetched August 2026) says Overviews and AI Mode may use query fan-out, and that a supporting link only needs the page indexed and snippet-eligible — **no additional technical requirements**. An Overview or Gemini citation is not a ChatGPT recommendation.

Claude is a fourth path. Anthropic's [web search tool](https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/agents-and-tools/tool-use/web-search-tool) docs (fetched August 2026) say Claude **determines when to search based on the prompt**, may search more than once, and cites sources in the final response. Tool-off = memory shortlist. Tool-on = read the citations.

Skip search-state and you will "fix proof" for a memory miss and "fix the homepage" for a retrieval miss. Record the engine and whether search ran. An Overview or Gemini cite is not a ChatGPT recommend.

## How do you check if ChatGPT or Perplexity mentions you at all?

**Run the same buyer prompts without your brand name, score mention vs citation vs recommendation, and treat "not mentioned" as a different loss than "mentioned, not picked."** There is no ChatGPT Search Console. You observe the answer.

The [15-minute audit](/blog/is-your-business-invisible-to-ai-how-to-run-a-15-minute-ai-visibility-audit) is the timed "are we invisible" triage. The [tracking post](/blog/how-to-track-when-ai-tools-cite-or-recommend-your-business) is the weekly panel. This section is the readout when a *rival* sits in the hire slot.

| Readout | What you write down | Mode it usually implies |
|---|---|---|
| **Omitted** | Your name never appears; rival is the pick | Missing proof, memory miss, or missing entity (check brand prompt) |
| **Mention-only** | Your name appears; you are not the pick | Proof or match; entity is probably fine |
| **Cited, not recommended** | Your URL is a source; rival is the advice | You are extractable; you are not shortlist-worthy |
| **Recommended** | You are the hire/buy option | This post is done — you are in a different job |
| **Mis-mentioned** | Wrong city, old DBA, namesake | Missing entity |

### Minimum mention check (diagnosis, not a new audit product)

Run these in ChatGPT (note whether Search ran) and Perplexity. Save the raw answer. Do not coach the model with your URL on the first pass.

1. **Brand sanity:** "Who is [exact public name]?" If this fails, do not trust a category miss as "the competitor is better at SEO."
2. **Category money prompt:** the sentence a buyer would type — city, job, constraint — with no brand.
3. **Rival-adjacent prompt:** "alternatives to [the name ChatGPT just used]."
4. **Same three in Perplexity.** Write the cited domains, not just the prose.

Score ChatGPT (search on/off), Perplexity, and optionally Gemini, Claude, and Google AI Overviews on mention / citation / recommendation, plus rival names and source domains.

A `chatgpt.com` click is not a mention. One lucky answer is not a diagnosis — re-run the same wording on another day or logged out. For a clock, use the 15-minute audit. For monthly rates, freeze a panel. For *why the rival won*, read the source list.

## Does a Wikipedia page explain a competitor win?

**Sometimes — as an entity-confidence and training-data signal — not as a placement product you can buy.** A Wikipedia article does not make ChatGPT recommend you. Missing one does not prove that is why you lost. The pattern it *can* explain: the rival is a documented public entity, and your reputation never left private channels.

The Wikimedia Foundation, on [November 10, 2025](https://wikimediafoundation.org/news/2025/11/10/in-the-ai-era-wikipedia-has-never-been-more-valuable/), called Wikipedia "one of the highest-quality datasets in the world for training AI" and said omitting it makes answers "significantly less accurate, less diverse, and less verifiable." That is a training-quality statement, not a ChatGPT ranking guide.

| Observation | Wikipedia might explain the win | Wikipedia is a distraction |
|---|---|---|
| Rival is a national brand or public company with a long article | Yes — knowledge-base miss is in play | — |
| Rival is a local shop with no article, but they own Angi / GBP / a listicle | No | Retrieval + proof |
| ChatGPT search is on and citations are Yelp + a "best of" post | No | Live retrieve, not the encyclopedia |
| Brand prompt already describes you correctly | Unlikely the blocker | You already have an entity; you lack proof |

Wikipedia's [notability guideline for organizations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Notability_(organizations_and_companies)) requires independent coverage in reliable sources — not a press release or your About page. If you cannot pass that bar, "get a Wikipedia page" is not this week's fix.

### How I test the Wikipedia hypothesis in an hour

1. Open the rival's article, if any. Note category, geography, and a lift-able first sentence.
2. Search your brand. Empty is normal for local operators. Empty plus a failed brand prompt is still an **entity** problem.
3. If ChatGPT's prose is a paraphrase of the rival's lead and search did not run, you have a memory / knowledge-base miss.
4. If search ran and Wikipedia is not cited, blame the cited domains.

Chase a Wikipedia-class entity only if you already have independent coverage that would satisfy notability, or if brand prompts hallucinate a namesake — not if you treat the page as a ChatGPT ad slot.

Wikimedia noted (same November 10, 2025 article) that Wikimedia projects are available in over 300 languages. Language-specific entity evidence can diverge. Cited directories still explain more competitor wins than the encyclopedia.

## What do you fix first this week if the rival keeps winning?

**Fix the mode you diagnosed — entity before proof, search-state before content volume, cited domains before a redesign.** This is one-week triage, not the get-recommended sequence. The 30/60/90 lives in the [Aug 6 playbook](/blog/how-to-get-chatgpt-and-perplexity-to-recommend-your-business). Internals live in the [mechanism post](/blog/how-chatgpt-and-perplexity-actually-decide-which-businesses-to-recommend). Pick the first repair that matches the screenshot.

| Diagnosed mode | First fix this week | What you refuse this week |
|---|---|---|
| **Missing entity** | Freeze one public name, phone, address, URL, category. Align site, Google Business Profile, top directories, and the About sentence. | New blog series, new brand campaign |
| **Missing proof** | List the domains ChatGPT / Perplexity already cited for the rival. Get *named* on one of those classes of surface. | Homepage animation, generic guest posts that never use your name |
| **Match miss** | Put the buyer's nouns on one page: job + city + customer type in sentence one. | Broader "thought leadership" |
| **Memory miss** | Re-test with Search forced. If the rival vanishes when search is on, you do not have a live-web loss — stop rewriting for a memory exam. | Paying for "ChatGPT SEO" that ignores search state |
| **Retrieval miss** | Become extractable *on the URLs in the citation list* — claim, correct, or earn a mention there. | Blocking crawlers "to be safe" without a written policy |
| **Knowledge-base miss** | Earn independent coverage a model can treat as an entity page. Wikipedia only if you already meet notability. | A stub you will have to defend |

### A one-week order of operations (diagnosis-shaped)

1. **Write the mode on the folder.** One sentence: "Retrieval miss — Perplexity cited Rival on Angi + a 2025 listicle; brand prompt passes."
2. **Confirm search state.** ChatGPT with Search on, ChatGPT with Search off if you can, Perplexity, and one Google AI Overviews query. Gemini if your buyers live there. Claude if they do.
3. **Entity 48 hours if the brand prompt failed.** No proof work until the string is stable.
4. **One proof surface if the brand prompt passed.** The surface the engine already drank from, not a new directory nobody cited.
5. **Re-run the same three prompts at the end of the week.** You are looking for a mode change (omitted → mention, mention → recommendation), not a miracle.

| End-of-week result | Meaning | Next week |
|---|---|---|
| Still omitted, brand prompt still wrong | You did not finish entity | Stay on identity; do not pitch editors |
| Still omitted, brand prompt clean | Proof has not landed or is on the wrong surface | One more cited-domain target |
| Mention, no recommendation | You entered the pile | Tighten "who we are for"; do not declare victory |
| Recommended on Perplexity, omitted on ChatGPT | Citation-forward engine moved; shortlist engine has not | Keep proof; re-test ChatGPT with Search on |

OpenAI does not publish a day-count SLA. Judge the week by whether the *mode* moved. If you cannot name the mode after one sitting, that is the audit I sell.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Does ChatGPT browse the web in real time and how does that affect me?

**ChatGPT can search the web, but not on every prompt — a search-off loss is a different diagnosis than a search-on loss.** OpenAI's [ChatGPT search](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/9237897-chatgpt-search) help page (fetched August 2026) says search may run when the question needs current information, that you can start it manually, and that it is available on Free, Go, Plus, Pro, Business, Enterprise, and Edu, including signed-out use. Search off: a famous rival can win from memory. Search on with rival citations: retrieval-and-proof. Log the state.

### How do I check if ChatGPT mentions my business?

**Ask buyer questions without your brand and score mention vs citation vs recommendation — there is no official ChatGPT mention feed.** Start with "Who is [name]?" so you do not confuse a missing entity with a competitor win. Then run the category prompt. A GA4 `chatgpt.com` session is a click, not a mention. Timed pass/fail: the [15-minute audit](/blog/is-your-business-invisible-to-ai-how-to-run-a-15-minute-ai-visibility-audit). Weekly rate: the [tracking method](/blog/how-to-track-when-ai-tools-cite-or-recommend-your-business).

### Does a Wikipedia page help ChatGPT recommend you?

**It can strengthen entity confidence because Wikimedia-class text is heavily used in training — it does not buy a recommendation slot.** On [November 10, 2025](https://wikimediafoundation.org/news/2025/11/10/in-the-ai-era-wikipedia-has-never-been-more-valuable/) the Wikimedia Foundation called Wikipedia among the highest-quality AI training datasets. That explains some national-brand memory wins. It does not override [organization notability](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Notability_(organizations_and_companies)). If search is on and citations are review sites, Wikipedia is not why you lost.

### How does Claude decide what to recommend?

**Claude recommends from training unless it decides the prompt needs the web; when it searches, it cites what it retrieved.** Anthropic's [web search tool](https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/agents-and-tools/tool-use/web-search-tool) docs (fetched August 2026) say Claude determines when to search, may search more than once, and cites sources. Tool-off versus tool-on — same fork as ChatGPT. Do not treat a Claude memory shortlist as a Perplexity retrieval miss. Architecture sits in the [mechanism post](/blog/how-chatgpt-and-perplexity-actually-decide-which-businesses-to-recommend).

### What is the best way to get Perplexity to link to my site?

**Read the domains Perplexity already cited on the prompt you lost, then become a page those systems can quote — named and linked are different wins.** If the rival got the link and you got silence, you are in a retrieval miss. Page-level citation work is [how to get Perplexity to cite your website as a source](/blog/how-to-get-perplexity-to-cite-your-website-as-a-source). First job here: write down the cited hosts.

### Does Perplexity use Google's index?

**Not as a documented Google-index feed.** In [Architecting and Evaluating an AI-First Search API](https://research.perplexity.ai/articles/architecting-and-evaluating-an-ai-first-search-api) (September 25, 2025), Perplexity says an early 2022 version used outside search APIs, that unofficial Google SERP-style APIs were too slow, and that they then built their own index — "over 200 billion unique URLs," crawled by PerplexityBot. I will not claim they never touch any Google-derived signal. I will claim they do not document organic answers as a resale of Google's index. Ranking #3 on Google is not a Perplexity diagnosis.

### Why does ChatGPT name a competitor when Google AI Overviews cite me?

**Different retrieval stacks.** An Overview citation means Google grounded on a snippet-eligible page; a ChatGPT recommendation means a chat model named a business. Google's [AI features](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/ai-features) page (fetched August 2026) ties Overviews and AI Mode to Search eligibility and query fan-out. Score both. Fix the engine you actually lost.

### What if Perplexity cites me and ChatGPT still names the rival?

**You are extractable on a retrieval engine and still losing the chat shortlist — usually missing proof or a memory miss, not a broken site.** Force Search on ChatGPT and read the citations. If your URL appears and the rival is still the pick, you are in mention-without-recommendation. Stay on proof and match. Do not rebuild the site for a citation you already earned.
