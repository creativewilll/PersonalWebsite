# What goes in the tells' place

Clean is not the same as good. After the scorer names a hit, rewrite from these rules.

## Dinner-table test

Read the paragraph out loud. If you would not say it to a founder at dinner, it is not ready.

## Specificity over adjectives

| Dead | Alive |
|---|---|
| We leverage industry-leading materials | We source from plants that test for wind, hail, and sun |
| Not just a tool, but a journey | The job finishes when the sheet updates |
| Trusted, reliable, and built to last | Six nails per shingle, every shingle |
| Many developers find Cursor powerful | Cursor's tab model still wins refactors; Claude Code wins long-horizon loops |

One specification beats three adjectives.

## Lead with the answer

No "in this article we'll explore." No "have you ever wondered." First sentence is the take, the receipt, or the unexpected fact.

## First person for William

William's blog is "I". Never corporate "we" unless the sentence is about the industry. Never "Sarah from Growth at HubSpot" energy.

## Receipts you may use (do not invent others)

- 600+ automations built / 500+ live (never flatten to "500+ built")
- 20,000+ hours architecting agentic systems
- 35,000+ hours saved for clients (separate from personal hours)
- Title: AI Systems Architect & Fractional AI CTO
- Org: Spurlock Studios LLC (public: Spurlock Studios)

If a claim needs a number you do not have, write it as an estimate with a date, or cut it.
