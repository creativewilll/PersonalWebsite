# Strategy Reference — Full Kallaway + heyDominik + Hormozi Encyclopedia

## 1. Trial Reels + Variation Testing (Kallaway + heyDominik)

### The Concept
Instagram's trial reels feature lets you test content with non-followers first.
Create 2–5 variations of the same core idea, changing only one variable per
variation. Post them within 24h of each other, wait 48h, then push the winner.

### Variables to Test (one per variation)
- Hook: spoken vs text-only vs visual-only
- First frame: talking-head vs product shot vs abstract visual
- CTA type: save vs comment keyword vs DM automation
- Length: 15s vs 30s vs 45s
- Music: trending audio vs original voiceover vs silence + text

### Decision Framework
After 48h, compare:
- Retention rate (% watched to end)
- Saves (strongest signal of value)
- Shares (strongest signal of virality)
- Follows gained per 1k views

Winner = highest saves + shares combo. Push to main feed.

## 2. Winner Reposting (Kallaway + heyDominik)

### Rules
- Top 10–20% of posts by engagement are "winners"
- First repost: unchanged, 2–4 weeks after original
- Second repost: new hook + same core, 6–8 weeks after original
- Third repost: different format (Reel → carousel or vice versa)
- Never repost more than 30% of weekly output

### Identification Criteria
- Views ≥ 5x your rolling 30-day median
- Save rate ≥ 3% of views
- Share rate ≥ 1% of views
- Follows gained ≥ 2% of views

## 3. Micro-Creator Pattern Mining (Kallaway)

### Process
1. Curate 10–20 accounts in your niche with 10k–50k followers
2. Weekly: scan their last 7 days of posts
3. Identify outliers: posts with 3x+ their average views
4. Deconstruct each outlier into components:
   - Visual hook (what you see in frame 1)
   - Spoken hook (first 1.5s of audio)
   - On-screen text (what text appears)
   - Story pattern (case study, list, confession, etc.)
   - POV (who is speaking and from what angle)
   - Retention mechanics (loops, reveals, cuts)
   - CTA type (save, share, comment, DM)
5. Use deconstructions as inspiration templates, never copy

### Key Insight
Small creators with viral outliers have found unmet demand. Their audience
overlap with yours means the same angle will likely work for you — with your
unique proof and perspective layered on top.

## 4. Visual + Text Hook Obsession (Kallaway + heyDominik)

### First Frame Rules
- Clean, uncluttered single subject
- High contrast between subject and background
- 3–5 words of on-screen text describing outcome or problem
- Text must be readable at phone-size in 0.5 seconds
- No logos, watermarks, or branding in first frame

### Hook Alignment
The spoken hook, visual hook, and on-screen text must all point at the same
promise. Misalignment = scroll. Example:
- Visual: MacBook with n8n canvas visible
- Text: "I automated my entire sales pipeline"
- Spoken: "This one workflow handles 47 leads a day without me touching it"

### Time Investment Rule (Kallaway)
Spend 80% of your creative energy on the first 1.5 seconds. The rest of the
video is delivery on the promise the hook makes.

## 5. Retention & Rewatch Loops (heyDominik)

### Drop-Off Patterns to Watch
- 0–1s: hook failure (visual not compelling enough)
- 3–5s: context too slow (get to the meat faster)
- 15–20s: teaching section too generic (needs specificity)
- Last 3s: weak ending (no reason to rewatch)

### Rewatch Triggers
- Open loop at start, close at end ("There's a third node that makes this 10x better...")
- Unexpected reveal at 70% mark
- Visual complexity that rewards second viewing
- Speed of information delivery (dense = rewatchable)

### Retention Note Template
For each post, log: timestamp of biggest drop, hypothesis why, and what to
test next time. Over 30 days this becomes your personal retention playbook.

## 6. Posting Frequency & Sustainability (Kallaway)

### Baseline for Growth (0-follower account)
- Minimum: 5 Reels/week
- Optimal for speed: 7–10 posts/week (Reels + carousels)
- Maximum sustainable: 2 posts/day

### Anti-Burnout Rules
- Batch filming: shoot 3–5 Reels in one session
- Batch editing: edit next day, schedule for the week
- Research day: 1 day/week dedicated to mining outliers
- Never change strategy mid-month — commit to 30 days minimum

### Consistency > Volume
Posting 5x/week every week for 6 months beats posting 14x/week for 3 weeks
then burning out. Build the habit first, then scale volume.

## 7. Multi-Format Cloning (Kallaway + heyDominik)

### One Idea → Multiple Formats
- Reel: 30–45s talking-head + screen recording
- Carousel: 7–10 slides with key points from the same idea
- Story: 3-part sequence teasing the Reel
- YouTube Short: same Reel with minor re-edit for different platform

### Cloning Rules
- Space formats 2–3 days apart (don't post Reel + carousel same day)
- Carousel should add depth the Reel couldn't cover
- Story sequence should create anticipation, not spoil

## 8. DM Automation Hooks (heyDominik)

### Frequency
- 1–2 dedicated DM-automation posts per week
- Additionally: weave DM CTAs into 30% of other posts

### Keyword Best Practices
- 1 word, ALL-CAPS, memorable
- Related to the value being offered
- Examples: AGENT, SYSTEM, BUILD, STACK, BLUEPRINT, RECEIPTS, MCP
- Never reuse the same keyword within 7 days

### Automation Flow
1. User comments keyword
2. ManyChat triggers DM with lead magnet link
3. DM includes follow-up question ("Want me to build yours?")
4. User reply = warm lead for consultation booking

### Caption Pattern for DM Posts
"[Hook line under 70 chars]

[2–3 body lines with specific proof]

Comment '[KEYWORD]' and I'll DM you [specific deliverable].

(Seriously — I built this for [avatar]. Takes 30 seconds to set up.)"

## 9. Ideal Viewer Clarity (Kallaway + heyDominik)

### Why It Matters
Every piece of content is a conversation with ONE person. If you're speaking
to everyone, you're speaking to no one. Define the avatar so specifically that
you can predict their exact objection to your hook.

### Avatar Components
- Identity: who they are (job title, company stage, daily reality)
- Pain: what keeps them up at night (specific, not generic)
- Desire: what they want in 6 months (specific outcome)
- Language: how they describe their problems (exact phrases)
- Platforms: where they spend time besides Instagram
- Objections: why they haven't solved this yet

### Threading Avatar Through Content
- Hooks should name the avatar's situation ("If you're a founder still manually...")
- Body should use their language
- CTA should promise their desired outcome
- Visual should show their world (laptop, office, tool they recognize)

## 10. Data Feedback Loop (heyDominik)

### Weekly Review (every Sunday)
1. Pull metrics for all posts from past 7 days
2. Sort by: views, saves, shares, follows gained
3. Identify top 2 performers and bottom 2
4. For top 2: what made them work? Log in retention notes.
5. For bottom 2: what failed? Was it hook, topic, or execution?
6. Adjust next week's calendar based on findings

### Monthly Review (end of each 30-day cycle)
1. Aggregate all performance data
2. Identify winning pillars (which topics consistently outperform)
3. Identify winning formats (Reel lengths, carousel styles)
4. Identify winning hook types
5. Double down on winners for next month's calendar
6. Drop or reframe consistently underperforming pillars

### Metrics That Matter (in order)
1. Saves (value signal)
2. Shares (virality signal)
3. Retention rate (content quality)
4. Follows gained per 1k views (niche fit)
5. Comments (engagement depth)
6. Views (reach, but vanity without the above)

## 11. Hormozi Value Equation (Applied to Content)

### The Formula
Value = (Dream Outcome × Perceived Likelihood of Achievement) ÷ (Time Delay × Effort & Sacrifice)

### Applied to Each Post
- Dream Outcome: what does the viewer get if they implement this?
- Perceived Likelihood: what proof do you show that it works?
- Time Delay: how fast can they get the result?
- Effort: how easy is the implementation?

### Content That Scores High
- "I built a $10k/mo pipeline with 3 n8n nodes" (high outcome, low effort)
- "Copy this exact workflow — takes 5 minutes" (low time delay, low effort)
- "Here's my actual dashboard showing 47 leads/day" (high likelihood via proof)

### Hormozi Mindset Rules for Content
- Volume: more posts = more data = faster learning = faster growth
- Leverage: one video can generate leads for months
- Boring works: the same 6 pillars, slightly different angle, every single day
- Receipts > credentials: show the result, not the certification
